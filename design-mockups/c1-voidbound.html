I

Talents

Shadow's talent rows are near-locked in Siege, and the value of each row swings with the fight rather than the tier. What follows is the default build, plus the two swaps worth carrying.

15

Void Tendrils

Useful for dealing with adds — the only row where a defensive pick outperforms.

45

Solace and Insanity

Strongest single-target option. Mind Flay: Insanity snapshots the channel.

75

Twist of Fate

Snipe low-health adds with Shadow Word: Pain and Death to hold uptime.

90

Halo

Default at 20+ yards. Divine Star on Thok and Garrosh.
